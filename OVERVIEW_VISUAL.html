The 5-Step QuantDiver Investment Process
01

Find Grade A stocks

Open Dashboard → Sectors tab. Look for Grade A signals (score ≥ 63). These are the setups with the strongest historical track record — 66.6% win rate at 90 days.

02

Check all six scores individually

Click the stock → Analysis tab. You want MOM high (momentum accelerating), RISK above 35 (balance sheet survivable), DEMAND high (sector tailwinds strong), INNOV high (R&D converting).

03

Check Market Condition

Dashboard → Market Intel tab. If FAVORABLE or NEUTRAL — proceed. If CAUTION — reduce position size. If RISK-OFF or CRISIS — wait. The best stock in a bad market still goes down.

04

Hold for 90–180 days

This is a medium-term conviction signal. Short-term volatility in the first 30 days is normal. The model is validated at 90 days (+19.68%) and even stronger at 180 days (+40.19%). Do not react to noise.

05

Watch for grade changes — not prices

Enable score alerts in Settings. If a Grade A stock drops to Grade C or D — that is the exit signal. Checking the price every day is not the process. The score change is the signal.
